Strike probability is estimated by randomly varying your target board at 15 ft inside each miss range and simulating many shots with the rigid-body model. A strike here means: pocket window at 60 ft, a useful entry angle, and the ball exits the back of the deck between the 8 and 9 pin spots. Colors: green ≥ 90%, yellow 50–90%, red < 45%. *Window is the widest miss range that still gives at least 50% strikes.
