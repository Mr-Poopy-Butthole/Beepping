1) Load video

Tip: A phone video from the side is best. Try to keep the camera steady and include lane boards/arrows if possible.

2) Calibration

Fast setup
Set ft/px by measuring a known lane length segment in the video (example: foul line to arrows ≈ 15 ft, foul line to pins 60 ft). Pause the video, then use any on-screen ruler/markup app to estimate pixels between those points.

If you don’t calibrate well, your mph will be off, but your pickup/breakpoint distances still help tune oil/friction.

Video viewer (click on the ball)

Workflow suggestion:
  1. Pause near release.
  2. Mode = Track ball center. Click the ball each frame for ~15-30 frames.
  3. Mode = Mark traction/pickup. Scrub to where it first noticeably “hooks” (end of skid) and click the ball once.
  4. Mode = Mark breakpoint. Scrub to max-outside point and click once.
  5. Copy outputs and paste into the simulator tab.
